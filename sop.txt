Rule 1: Latency > 150 ms AND packet loss > 2%
Cause: link saturation from background traffic
Action: throttle QoS Class 4, reroute critical voice/data over secondary SD-WAN

Rule 2: Packet loss > 10%
Cause: faulty cable or interface
Action: check the interface, replace the cable, switch to the backup link

Rule 3: Jitter > 30 ms
Cause: congestion or unstable link
Action: prioritise voice traffic, check the QoS policy

Rule 4: Node status = down
Cause: device or power failure
Action: check power, restart the device, escalate to the on-site team

Rule 5: Latency < 100 ms AND packet loss < 1%
Cause: none, link is healthy
Action: no action needed
